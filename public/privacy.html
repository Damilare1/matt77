Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Matt77, we collect the following information:

  • •Telegram User ID — Your unique Telegram identifier, used to manage your session and authenticate requests.
  • •Message Content — Text messages, voice messages, audio files, and images you send to the bot. These are processed by AI models to generate responses.
  • •Session Data — Conversation context, token usage counters, and model preferences stored for the duration of your session.
  • •Name — Your first and last name as provided by Telegram, used for personalized greetings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • •Processing your messages through AI models to generate responses, images, videos, and transcriptions.
  • •Maintaining conversation context within your session to provide coherent multi-turn conversations.
  • •Managing your token usage and session preferences.
  • •Managing calendar events on your behalf when you use the calendar feature.

3. Third-Party Services

Matt77 relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • •OpenAI API — When you use an OpenAI model, your messages are sent to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI's privacy policy applies to this processing.
  • •Anthropic API — When you use a Claude model, your messages are sent to Anthropic's API. Anthropic's privacy policy applies.
  • •Google AI & Google Calendar APIs — When you use a Gemini/Imagen model or the calendar feature, data is processed through Google's APIs. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • •Brave Search API — When a request requires up-to-date information, your search query is sent to the Brave Search API. Brave's privacy policy applies.
  • •Amazon DynamoDB — Session data is stored in AWS DynamoDB. Amazon's privacy policy applies.
  • •Telegram Bot API — All interactions occur through Telegram's platform. Telegram's privacy policy applies.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Session data (conversation context, token usage, preferences) is stored in DynamoDB and persists for the duration of your active session. Temporary files (audio downloads, generated files) are deleted immediately after processing.

We do not permanently store your message content beyond the active session context. You can clear your session data at any time using the /clearcontext command.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections to all third-party services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • •Clear your conversation data at any time using /clearcontext.
  • •Stop using the bot at any time by blocking it on Telegram.
  • •Request deletion of your stored data by contacting us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of Matt77 after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out through our Telegram bot.
