Getting Started

Matt77 is a Telegram bot powered by leading AI models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. To get started:

  1. Open Telegram and search for Matt77, or use the direct link.
  2. Send /start to initialize your session.
  3. Start sending messages. Matt77 will respond with AI-generated answers.

Your session includes a token allocation. Use /stats to check your remaining balance.

Commands

Command Description
/start Begin a new session with the bot
/stats View your monthly token usage and video quota
/getconfig View all your current settings
/support Log a complaint or support ticket
/clearcontext Clear conversation history to start fresh
/compact Summarise and compress conversation history
/listmodels List all available AI models
/setgptmodel · /getgptmodel Switch or show the current chat model
/setimgmodel · /getimgmodel Switch or show the image generation model
/setvideomodel · /getvideomodel Switch or show the video generation model
/setaudiomodel · /getaudiomodel Switch or show the audio transcription model
/voice_prompts Toggle treating voice notes as AI prompts

AI Chat

Simply send a text message to Matt77 and it will respond using the currently selected AI model. The bot maintains conversation context across messages within your session, allowing for natural multi-turn conversations.

To reset the conversation context and start a new topic, use /clearcontext.

Matt77 acts as an agent — it can autonomously chain multiple tool calls to complete complex tasks. For example, asking "Search for the latest news on a topic and write me a summary" will trigger multiple steps automatically.

Image Generation & Editing

Ask Matt77 to generate images and it will use your selected image model — OpenAI GPT Image or Google Imagen — to create them. Simply describe what you want:

"Generate an image of a sunset over a mountain lake"

To edit an existing image, send the image as a photo with a caption describing the desired changes. The bot will use the image as a base and apply your edits.

Internet Search

Matt77 can search the internet for up-to-date information. When you ask about recent events, news, or anything that requires current data, the bot will automatically search the web and include relevant sources in its response.

"What are the latest developments in AI?"

You can also ask it to research specific topics, compare products, look up facts, or find current prices and availability. The bot returns answers with source links so you can verify the information.

Video Generation

Matt77 can generate videos using OpenAI's Sora model. Ask for a video and the bot will start the generation process:

"Create a 4-second video of waves crashing on a beach"

Videos can be 4, 8, or 12 seconds long. You can also generate videos from an image by sending a photo with a prompt describing the desired animation.

Video generation takes time. The bot will give you a request ID that you can use to check the status of your video at any time.

Voice & Audio

Send a voice message or audio file to Matt77 and it will transcribe the content using your selected transcription model (Whisper, GPT-4o Transcribe, or Gemini). You can optionally add a caption to provide context for the transcription. Use /voice_prompts to toggle whether voice notes are treated as AI prompts.

Supported formats include all common audio formats that Telegram supports (OGG, MP3, WAV, etc.).

Calendar Management Work in progress

Matt77 can manage Google Calendar events through natural language. Examples:

  • • "Schedule a team meeting tomorrow at 2pm for 1 hour"
  • • "What's on my calendar this week?"
  • • "Move the 2pm meeting to 3pm"
  • • "Cancel the team meeting"

The bot supports full CRUD operations: creating, listing, updating, and deleting events.
